Keyword board ·

Keyword opportunity board, relevance first

Every keyword in the universe, scored. Relevance is how close the phrase is to what this app does. Demand is how often Play's own autocomplete offers the phrase and how high. Competition is the installs behind the top ten. Priority puts relevance first: a phrase nobody searches for is worthless, but so is a phrase this app cannot honestly answer.
